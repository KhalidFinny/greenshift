and the shared `@greenshift/ui` layer.

`packages/ui/src/layouts/role-shell.tsx:212-282`

### F-20 Landing motion contradicts the declared MOTION dial

`DESIGN.md` sets MOTION 1: hover and state transitions only, no scroll
choreography, no loops. Four landing behaviours do not hold that:

| Where | What | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `components/atoms/FloatingPill.tsx` | `animate-float` / `-delayed` / `-slow` / `-slow-delayed`, 3s and 4s `ease-in-out` loops | perpetual, no user trigger |
| `components/organisms/EcosystemSection.tsx:110-118` | `requestAnimationFrame` orbit loop, runs for the life of the page | perpetual, no user trigger |
| `components/organisms/HeroSection.tsx:20-22` | three scroll-parallax layers (`useParallax`) | scroll choreography |
| `components/organisms/HowItWorksSection.tsx:38` | mouse parallax on the image | pointer choreography |

The first two are the clearest: the antislop-ui "Endless Pulses and Loops" entry
says perpetual motion is noise that never lets the user rest, and R-19 requires
motion to have a stated UX purpose. The parallax is a softer call, since
`CLAUDE.md` documents it as the landing identity.

The class name is built dynamically (`animate-${animation}`), so this does not
show up in a plain grep for `animate-float`: it is genuinely applied.

**This is an owner decision, not a silent fix.** Per R-37 I have not overridden
documented direction. Options: relax the dial (landing is MOTION 2 while the app
surfaces stay MOTION 1), or remove the loops and parallax to match MOTION 1.

Rule **R-19**, against the MOTION 1 dial.
